FY 2024 Incentive Payment Pool Amount

DCL-23-14

Publication Date: November 30, 2023
Current as of: May 22, 2025

DEAR COLLEAGUE LETTER

DCL-23-14

DATE: November 30, 2023

TO: State IV-D Directors

SUBJECT: FY 2024 Incentive Payment Pool Amount

Dear Colleague:

The amount of the incentive payment pool for fiscal year 2024 is $713,414,242.

In accordance with the provisions of 42 U.S.C. 658a, incentives are based on child support collections and state performance levels on the five federal performance measures. Data must be complete and reliable, as determined by data reliability audits.

If you have questions, please contact us at DPSAsupport@acf.hhs.gov.

Sincerely,

Tanguler Gray
Commissioner
